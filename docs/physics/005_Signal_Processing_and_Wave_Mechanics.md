## Wave Models and Domain Translation

Signal processing is a mathematical approach used to remove noise from electroencephalograms (EEG) or audio signals to identify the true underlying source.

### [Rosetta Stone: Translation of Physical Variables]

* **Phase Drift (Lag):** The "timing discrepancy" between waves. Natural business (e.g., sales and collections) always has a certain lag.
* **Resonant Frequency:** The "periodicity" hidden within the system. An unnatural cycle different from a normal 30-day cycle (e.g., every 7.2 days) suggests the presence of automated bots.
* **Fractal Noise ($1/f$ Fluctuation / Pink Noise):** The "natural fluctuation" inherent to the natural world and human behavior. In contrast, something completely random is called "White Noise."

### Detection of Artificial Fabrication (White-Noising)

Healthy data always draws the "Pink Noise (downward sloping decay line)" unique to the natural world. However, humans are absolutely incapable of creating "natural fluctuations" by thinking about them. When an accountant manually enters thousands of fabricated journal entries to hide losses, the data structure mathematically mutates into pure random **"White Noise (a flat line)."** This is definitive proof of tampering based not on the magnitude of numbers, but on the "texture" of the numbers.
