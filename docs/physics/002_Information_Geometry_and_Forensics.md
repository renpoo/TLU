# 002. Information Geometry & Forensics

## 🔬 Conclusion: Is the Data Distorted into an Unknown Dimension (Criminal Distribution)?

The ultimate conclusion of the "Information Geometry" phase in TLU is **to mathematically prove whether the data in the network follows the normal probability distribution established in the past, or if it has been dragged into an "unknown dimension ignoring past physical laws (a realm of crime or tampering)" by someone's hands.**

Clever frauds that can never be detected by traditional, one-dimensional bookkeeping like "do debits and credits match?" clearly reveal themselves as "geometric spatial distortions" in the data structure.

## Geometric Models and Domain Translation

Information geometry treats probability distributions as points on a "manifold (curved surface)" and measures the "distance" and "distortion" between distributions.

### [Rosetta Stone: Translation of Physical/Mathematical Variables]

* **Z-Score (Residual / Mass Collapse):** The degree of deviation from the macro accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Equity). If this jumps over the threshold, it means the "Law of Conservation of Mass of the entire system has collapsed" due to unauthorized fund leakage (embezzlement, etc.) off the books.
* **KL Divergence (Information-Geometric Mutation / KL Drift):** The degree of deviation from past behavioral patterns (probability distributions). Even if the total transaction volume is normal, if an "abnormally high frequency of remittances to a partner rarely dealt with" occurs, the curvature of the manifold sharply distorts, and the KL Drift maximizes.
* **Manifold Dimensionality:** The complexity of the network (Effective Rank). If this dimension suddenly plummets, it means all resources in the system have been sucked into a single loop (like wash trading).

### Macro Spatial Distortion and Colluding Groups

In cases of "Wash Trading" or "Money Laundering (Sample 7)" conducted not by a lone wolf but by a colluding group, the overall mass is conserved, so the Z-Score shows a normal value. However, their "abnormal closed transaction loop" is visualized in the 3D KL Drift space as a **"gigantic abnormal mountain ridge rising from a calm sea (deviation to an unknown distribution),"** completely unmasking the outline of the colluding group.

## Identifying the Crime Route (Topology)

After detecting spatial distortion (macro), TLU zooms into the network topology to pinpoint "which physical pipe (transaction route)" is generating that distortion.

### Abnormal Topological Structure (Network Topology)

* In normal business processes, funds flow through authorized routes. However, when fraud like Wash Trading (Sample 1) occurs, a "thick, bright red shortcut (abnormal topological line)" is formed between nodes that should not originally connect. This is the "crime scene" itself—the source of the information-geometric distortion.

## 🔬 Falsifiability and Model Limits

The information geometry engine asserts **strict geometric facts** such as "the Law of Conservation of Mass of the entire system has collapsed (Z-Score anomaly)" or "a specific group is taking actions that completely ignore past probability distributions (KL Drift anomaly)."
However, geometric data alone cannot determine whether the cause of that abnormal distribution is "market manipulation by a criminal syndicate" or a "legitimate mutation due to launching a massive new business division and starting trade with new partners."

An auditor who detects an abnormal ridge of KL Drift must narrow down to the specific group of accounts pointed out by the system and conduct a field audit (additional verification) to determine "what new contractual relationship exists among them."
