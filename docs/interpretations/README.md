## 🎨 Basics of Reading Graphs: A Top-Down Approach from the Big Picture to the Details

Just as you sketch the overall composition of a painting before drawing in the details, you should read TLU's graphs by increasing the resolution from the **"Whole (Macro Terrain)"** to the **"Local (Micro Spikes)."**

### 1. Macro Perspective: Reading the Terrain (Waves) and Trends

* **How to Look:** Take a bird's-eye view of the gentle undulations and trends of the entire graph.
* **What you Learn:** Understand normal seasonal variations like "funds are piling up overall towards the end of the term (swelling of waves)," or structural changes like "the entire system is gradually heading towards a thermodynamic death (infinite increase of frictional heat)."

### 2. Micro Perspective: Reading Abnormal "Spikes (Needles)" and "Ruptures"

* **How to Look:** Search for "sharp needles (spikes)" suddenly protruding from a smooth terrain, or "ruptures (blanks)" where strong connections suddenly disappear.
* **What you Learn:** Identify single-shot anomalies such as "A massive mass deficit deviating from the normal trend has occurred only on this specific day, in this specific account"—indicating embezzlement, fictitious recordings, or pinpoint cerebral infarctions (blood flow blockage).

### 3. Identifying Fraud by Combining Perspectives

* **"The terrain is calm, but there is a sharp spike"** = High probability of a concealed, single-shot fraud or error (e.g., embezzlement, input mistake).
* **"There are no spikes, but the entire terrain is unnaturally diverging/running wild"** = High probability of a sustained, structural hijacking of the entire system, such as circular trading (wash trading) or window dressing using the whole organization.
