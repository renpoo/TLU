## Confirming Profit Erosion and Momentum

After confirming overall health via the B/S, we use the P/L to identify "how daily business generates profit, and where it is being scraped away."

### Profit and Loss (P/L) Waterfall

![Sample 0 P/L Waterfall](../../samples/Sample_0_Healthy/readme_plots/000_0_1__PL_Waterfall_Total.png)

* **📊 Visual Structure**: A cascade step chart showing the flow from revenue, minus various expenses, down to the final net income.
* **🚨 Anomaly Detection**: Look for a disproportionately massive "red expense block (a spike in a specific expense)" compared to other expense items. If a massive red block suddenly appears in a specific month, an immediate audit focused on that account (investigation into sudden waste or fraud) is necessary.

### Profit and Loss (P/L) Trend

![Sample 0 P/L Trend](../../samples/Sample_0_Healthy/readme_plots/000_0_1__PL_Trend.png)

* **📊 Visual Structure**: A time-series bar chart (Revenue/Expenses) and a line chart (Net Income).
* **🚨 Anomaly Detection**: Look for places where only the red expense bars suddenly jump up (an unexplained spike) during a period when the green revenue bars (Sales) are flat or declining. This is a sign that the organization has lost flexibility and is suffering under the structural weight of fixed costs.
