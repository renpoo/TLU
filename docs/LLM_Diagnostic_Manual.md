## 2. Principle of Comparative Synthesis

After establishing primary findings, do not list them in isolation. You must **cross-reference and weigh (比較衡量)** findings to synthesize a unified "Meta-Diagnosis."

### 2.1 Discarding Superficial Positives
*   **Rule:** Always prioritize deep physical metrics (thermodynamics, stiffness, residuals) over superficial statistics (revenue volume, static asset growth, or temporary Z-score calm).
*   **Example:** If basic statistics show "revenue is growing healthy," but thermodynamics shows "free energy is depleted ($F < -0.10$)" and forensics shows a non-zero residual (leakage), reject the healthy growth. Diagnose the revenue growth as **"Metabolic Bloat / Camouflaged Hemorrhage"** and set the final judgment to "Critical."

### 2.2 Synthesizing Contradictions
*   **Rule:** When different filters present contradictory signals, check if they describe a "Composite Pathology."
*   **Example:** If control theory shows "active flow with $\rho \ge 1.0$," but forensics shows "Edge Stress is `0.00`." Resolve this contradiction as **"Recirculation Lock (Gridlock)"**—meaning siphoned flows are running in a futile circle because the primary channels are severed.

### 2.3 Strict Terminology Integration
*   **Rule:** When referencing graphs, use the exact labels, axis names, and physical variables present in the visual output (e.g., `relative_leak_ratio`, `spectral_radius`, `local_grad_t`) rather than loose translations.

### 2.4 Overcoming Model Pollution (Boiled Frog Syndrome)
*   **Rule:** Avoid relying solely on rolling Z-score stats, which adapt to chronic anomalies and decay over time. Prioritize physical invariants (Kirchhoff residual, spectral radius $\rho$, stiffness lock) to detect ongoing long-term pathologies.

### 2.5 Statistical False Positive Triaging
*   **Rule:** Dismiss statistical Z-score alerts (e.g., July Z-score peaking at `4.90` in Sample 0) as seasonal fluctuations if physical conservation residuals remain exactly `0.00` and spectral radius is `0.00`.

### 2.6 Interlocking with Physical Verification (Falsifiability)
*   **Rule:** Connect your verification conditions to **independent third-party physical evidence outside the database boundary**.
    *   *Examples:* For money leakage, request "original bank statement passbooks or banking API logs." For wash trades, request "third-party logistics shipping slips (e.g., FedEx, UPS, DHL original documents) or delivery receipts."
